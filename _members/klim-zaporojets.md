Klim is a computer scientist with a PhD from Ghent University, specializing in natural language processing, information extraction, machine learning, and knowledge graphs. His research focuses on developing algorithms and datasets for extracting information from text and using it to expand and update existing knowledge, with broader interests in large language models and multimodal learning. Before joining the Hulman Lab, he was a postdoctoral researcher at the Department of Computer Science at Aarhus University, where he held a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Postdoctoral Fellowship. He is particularly motivated by developing practical AI solutions that address real needs in diabetes research and care. 

At Steno Diabetes Center Aarhus, he aims to work closely with clinicians, including doctors, nurses, and other healthcare professionals, to understand the challenges they face and develop tools that help them work more effectively, improve patient care, and ultimately benefit people living with diabetes.
