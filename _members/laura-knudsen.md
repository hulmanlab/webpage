Laura holds a bachelor’s degree in Public Health Science from Aarhus University and is currently enrolled in the master’s degree in the same field. She works with the Hulman Lab team on a scoping review of AI tools for the diagnosis and treatment of diabetes. She also contributes to administrative tasks and communication activities within the group.
She has broad interests spanning global health, epidemiology, and the digital transformation of healthcare and health-related behaviour. In her bachelor’s project, she developed a health literacy index using routinely collected data from the Demographic and Health Surveys in low- and middle-income countries. She is currently developing the project into a scientific article. A key part of the project was exploring how digital capabilities could be incorporated into the index, enabling digital health literacy to be studied in relation to health outcomes across many low- and middle-income countries.
