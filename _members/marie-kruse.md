Marie is currently pursuing a master’s degree in Public Health Science at Aarhus University. She is a teaching assistant for the course AI in Public Health, where she teaches students about the development, implementation, and evaluation of AI models in healthcare, with particular emphasis on predictive performance, fairness, and ethical considerations. She is preparing for a research stay at Imperial College London, where she will collaborate on two research projects. The first will evaluate the predictive performance and fairness of AI-based ECG models in the UK Biobank cohort, focusing on differences across socioeconomic, demographic, and disease subgroups. The second will investigate how fairness metrics have been reported in AI-based clinical prediction studies following the introduction of TRIPOD+AI. Her research interests include epidemiology, clinical risk prediction, artificial intelligence, and the equitable implementation of AI in healthcare.
